<idea>
**Design Movement**: "Cyber-Brutalist Noir"
**Core Principles**:
1. **Raw Functionality**: Expose the structural grid and layout lines.
2. **High Contrast**: Pure black (#000000) vs. Neon Purple (#8B5CF6) and Stark White (#FFFFFF).
3. **Data-First**: Information density is a feature, not a bug.
**Color Philosophy**: A deep, void-like black background represents the "Midnight" theme, punctuated by sharp, electric purple accents that signify the "Dev" energy. It's serious, technical, and slightly edgy.
**Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric grid systems. Content blocks are not centered but offset, creating dynamic tension.
**Signature Elements**:
1. **Visible Grid Lines**: Thin, faint borders separating sections.
2. **Monospace Typography**: For headers and data points, reinforcing the coding nature.
3. **Glitch Effects**: Subtle hover states that mimic digital interference.
**Interaction Philosophy**: Interactions are instant and sharp. No soft fades; things snap into place.
**Animation**: "Terminal" style typing effects for text. Elements slide in with a hard ease-in-out.
**Typography System**:
- Headers: 'JetBrains Mono' or 'Space Mono' (Monospace)
- Body: 'Inter' or 'Switzer' (Clean Sans-serif) for readability against the raw headers.
</idea>

<idea>
**Design Movement**: "Ethereal Glassmorphism"
**Core Principles**:
1. **Depth & Layering**: Use blur and transparency to create a sense of deep space.
2. **Fluidity**: Elements float and drift rather than sit statically.
3. **Soft Glows**: Light comes from within elements, not just external sources.
**Color Philosophy**: Deep midnight blues and purples (gradients) rather than solid black. It feels like looking into a nebula—mysterious but inviting.
**Layout Paradigm**: Floating cards. Content is contained in translucent "glass" panels that hover over a moving background.
**Signature Elements**:
1. **Glass Cards**: heavy backdrop-filter: blur() with thin, semi-transparent white borders.
2. **Aurora Gradients**: Animated background blobs of color that shift slowly.
3. **Rounded Corners**: Everything is soft and pill-shaped (2xl or 3xl radius).
**Interaction Philosophy**: Interactions are fluid and organic. Hovering causes elements to lift and glow brighter.
**Animation**: Parallax scrolling effects. Elements fade and float up gently.
**Typography System**:
- Headers: 'Plus Jakarta Sans' (Geometric, modern)
- Body: 'DM Sans' (Approachable, rounded)
</idea>

<idea>
**Design Movement**: "Swiss International Tech"
**Core Principles**:
1. **Typographic Hierarchy**: Size and weight dictate importance, not decoration.
2. **Grid Precision**: Strict adherence to a 12-column grid.
3. **Negative Space**: Massive margins to let the content breathe.
**Color Philosophy**: "Midnight" is interpreted as a very dark, desaturated slate/navy (#0F172A). Accents are functional and restrained (e.g., a single electric blue or violet for CTAs).
**Layout Paradigm**: Split-screen layouts and strong vertical axes.
**Signature Elements**:
1. **Oversized Typography**: Hero text that spans the entire viewport width.
2. **Rule Lines**: Thick horizontal separators between sections.
3. **Minimalist Icons**: Stroke-only icons, no fills.
**Interaction Philosophy**: deliberate and weighty. Scroll smoothing is essential.
**Animation**: Staggered reveals. Text reveals line-by-line from a mask.
**Typography System**:
- Headers: 'Helvetica Now Display' or 'Unica One' (Strong, neutral)
- Body: 'Roboto' or 'Satoshi' (Neutral, legible)
</idea>
