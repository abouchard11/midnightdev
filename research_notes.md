## Initial Findings: ChatGPT Search Optimization (GEO)

### Definition
**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**, also referred to as "Answer Engine Optimization" (AEO) or "ChatGPT SEO", is the process of optimizing online presence to rank in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, SearchGPT).

### Key Differences from Traditional SEO
-   **Goal:** Instead of ranking 10 blue links, the goal is to be "cited" or "recommended" in the single AI-generated answer.
-   **Content Style:** Favors "smart, conversational content" that directly answers questions rather than keyword-stuffed pages.
-   **Authority:** Heavily relies on brand mentions, citations, and structured data across the web, as AI models "read" the whole web to form an opinion.

### Local Business Application
-   **Mirroring Local SEO:** ChatGPT often mirrors Google Maps/Local Pack data. Optimizing Google Business Profile (GBP) is still crucial.
-   **Citations & Reviews:** Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) and high-quality reviews across directories help AI "trust" the business existence and quality.
-   **Structured Data:** Schema markup (which we just implemented!) is vital for AI to parse specific services, hours, and location data.

### Common Themes & "The Playbook"
-   **Schema is King:** All agencies agree that structured data (JSON-LD) is the #1 technical requirement for AI visibility.
-   **Citations Matter:** Being mentioned on other authoritative sites (even without links) helps AI models "learn" about the business.
-   **Q&A Format:** Content should be structured as direct answers to common questions (FAQ style).
-   **Local = Trust:** For local businesses, the "trust" signals (reviews, consistent NAP) are even more critical for AI than for Google.
